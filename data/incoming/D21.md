# Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant Guidelines

Version 3. Issued by the Grants Policy branch, Department of Regional Recovery, 3 August 2026. This version replaces version 2 of 15 May 2026.

## What has changed

The Minister has agreed to extend the closing date and to give renters access to help with lost contents. All other rules are restated unchanged.

## Eligibility

To be eligible, a household must have a combined annual income below $120,000.

The grant is available to owner-occupiers, and renters are also eligible for the $2,000 contents tier.

The damaged property must be the household's principal place of residence and must be located within the declared Riverbend disaster area.

## Grant amounts

The grant is paid in three tiers: $2,000 for loss of contents, $5,000 for minor structural damage and $10,000 for major structural damage.

Renters may apply for the contents tier only, as repairs to the building remain the responsibility of the owner.

## Applications

Applications close on 31 December 2026.

Applicants must provide proof of address in the form of a utility bill or a lease.

No fee is charged to apply for the grant or to have an application assessed.

Approved grants are paid by electronic funds transfer into the applicant's own bank account.

## Earlier enquiries

Renters who were told before 3 August 2026 that they could not apply may lodge an application under these guidelines.

## Review

Applicants who disagree with a decision may request an internal review within 28 days. These guidelines will be reviewed in August 2027, or earlier if the program changes.
