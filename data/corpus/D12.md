# Remote community applications procedure

Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant. Regional Operations, Riverbend Regional Office. Declared procedure, 20 April 2026.

## Purpose

Many households in the remote communities of the Riverbend region do not receive utility bills in their own name and do not hold a written lease. Housing is often shared, provided through community arrangements or paid for by another family member. This procedure sets out how Riverbend Regional Office staff accept proof of address from these households so that they are not excluded from the grant.

## Who this applies to

This procedure applies to applicants living in the remote communities listed in the attachment held with this record.

## Accepted proof of address

Applicants in remote communities may provide a statutory declaration or a letter from a community organisation as proof of address instead of a utility bill or lease.

A letter from a community organisation must be on the organisation's letterhead, name the applicant and the address, and be signed by an office holder. A statutory declaration must be witnessed by an authorised witness, and Regional Office staff can arrange this during outreach visits.

## Processing

Regional Office staff upload the document with the application and note in the case management system that it was accepted under this procedure. Assessment then proceeds in the normal way.

## Contact

Questions about this procedure should be directed to the Regional Operations manager at the Riverbend Regional Office.
