# Flood recovery grants still open

Riverbend Recovery News, June 2026. Prepared by the Communications branch of the Department of Regional Recovery.

Three months after the floods, the Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant is helping thousands of local households rebuild. More than 5,000 grants have already been paid, and there is still time to apply.

Eligible households will receive $2,000 to replace lost contents, $5,000 where the home has minor structural damage, or $10,000 where the damage is major.

The grant is available to owner-occupiers only.

Applications close on 31 October 2026.

Applying is free and can be done online or by calling the grant hotline. You will need to show that you lived at the damaged property, and an assessment officer may contact you to arrange an inspection.

## Stories from the community

At the Riverbend showgrounds, volunteers have helped families sort donated furniture and household goods. Local councils have also cleared more than 2,000 tonnes of flood debris from streets and parks.

## Where to get help

If you need help with your application, call the grant hotline or visit the Riverbend Regional Office. Support is also available through the recovery support line for anyone who is finding things hard.
