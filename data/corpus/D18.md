# Grants Steering Committee minutes

Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant. Meeting held 10 May 2026, Head Office.

Present: the Chair (Deputy Secretary, Regional Recovery), the Director of Grants Policy, the Chief Finance Officer, the Director of Customer Service and a representative of the Riverbend Regional Office. Secretariat: Grants Policy.

## Item 1: Minutes of the previous meeting

The minutes of the meeting of 12 April 2026 were accepted as a true record.

## Item 2: Income threshold

The Director of Grants Policy presented analysis showing that many flood-affected households earning between $90,000 and $120,000 were struggling with higher rents, insurance excesses and the rising cost of building materials.

The committee decided to raise the household income threshold from $90,000 to $120,000 because of cost-of-living pressures in the Riverbend region.

Grants Policy will issue version 2 of the guidelines to give effect to this decision, and Customer Service will update hotline scripts and reference material.

## Item 3: Payment times

The Chief Finance Officer reported that the average time to payment was 26 days. The committee asked Finance to aim for 21 days by the end of July.

## Item 4: Other business

The Riverbend representative noted that some remote communities were finding the application process difficult. The committee asked the Regional Office to brief Customer Service at the next meeting.

Next meeting: 14 June 2026.
