# Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant: frequently asked questions

Published on the staff intranet by the Communications branch, March 2026.

## What is the grant for?

The grant helps households in the Riverbend region recover from the February 2026 floods by contributing to repairs and to the replacement of lost household contents.

## Who can apply?

To be eligible, a household must have a combined annual income below $90,000.

Only owner-occupiers can apply, so the grant is not open to people who rent the home they live in.

The home must be the household's main residence and must be inside the declared disaster area.

## How much is the grant?

The amount depends on the level of damage, which is assessed by the department after the application is lodged.

## How do I help someone who wants to apply?

Direct callers and visitors to the online application form or to the grant hotline. Staff outside the Customer Service branch should not try to assess eligibility themselves, as the rules can be complex.

## What if someone is upset?

Many people affected by the floods are under great stress. Listen, be patient and offer the number for the recovery support line.

## Where can I find more information?

The full guidelines are held in the department's records system. Contact the Grants Policy branch with any policy questions, or the Communications branch about this FAQ.
