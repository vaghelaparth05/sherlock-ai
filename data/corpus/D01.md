# Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant Guidelines

Version 1. Issued by the Grants Policy branch, Department of Regional Recovery, 2 March 2026.

## Purpose

These guidelines set out the rules for the Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant, which helps households in the Riverbend region repair their homes and replace what was lost in the February 2026 floods. They apply to all staff who assess, administer or answer enquiries about the grant.

## Eligibility

To be eligible, a household must have a combined annual income below $90,000.

The grant is available to owner-occupiers only.

The damaged property must be the household's principal place of residence and must be located within the declared Riverbend disaster area.

## Grant amounts

The grant is paid in three tiers: $2,000 for loss of contents, $5,000 for minor structural damage and $10,000 for major structural damage.

The tier is set by an assessment officer after reviewing photographs, receipts and, where needed, an inspection of the property.

## Applications

Applications close on 31 October 2026.

Applicants must provide proof of address in the form of a utility bill or a lease.

Assessment officers will confirm identity, ownership and income before a grant is approved. Applicants who disagree with a decision may request an internal review within 28 days.

## Contact

Enquiries should be directed to the grant hotline, which is staffed by the Customer Service branch. Policy questions from staff should be sent to the Grants Policy branch.
