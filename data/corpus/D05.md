# Training slides: Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant basics

Draft prepared by the Learning and Development branch for new hotline staff, April 2026.

## Slide 1: Welcome

This module introduces the Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant and the role of hotline staff in helping callers apply. It takes about twenty minutes to complete.

## Slide 2: Why the grant exists

The February 2026 floods damaged thousands of homes across the Riverbend region. The grant helps households repair their homes and replace essential items.

## Slide 3: The income test

To be eligible, a household must have a combined annual income below $90,000.

Trainer note: remind participants that income is checked by assessment officers, not by hotline staff.

## Slide 4: Your role on the hotline

Hotline staff explain the rules, help callers start an application and record each call in the case management system. They do not approve or refuse applications.

## Slide 5: Looking after yourself

Calls about the floods can be distressing. Take short breaks between difficult calls and talk to your team leader if you need support. The Employee Assistance Program is available to all staff and their families.

## Slide 6: Quiz

Check your understanding with the five-question quiz at the end of this module. You need four correct answers to complete the module.
