# Call centre script: Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant (version 2)

Prepared by the Customer Service branch for grant hotline staff, 25 May 2026. Updated for Guidelines version 2.

## Opening

Thank the caller for contacting the Department of Regional Recovery and confirm they are calling about the Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant. Ask for the caller's name, a contact number and the address of the damaged property.

## Checking eligibility

Read the eligibility rules to the caller as written.

Households with a combined annual income below $120,000 can apply.

The grant is available to owner-occupiers only.

If the caller rents their home, explain that they are not eligible under the current guidelines.

## Proof of address

Every applicant must provide a current utility bill or a lease showing the damaged property's address as proof of address.

If the caller does not have either document, explain that the application cannot proceed until they provide one.

## Ending the call

Offer to send the application link by text message or email. Remind the caller to keep receipts and photographs of the damage. If the caller is distressed, offer the number for the recovery support line.

## Escalation

Transfer the call to a team leader if the caller wishes to make a complaint or reports suspected fraud. Record every call in the case management system before closing it.
