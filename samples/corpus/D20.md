# Field visit report: remote communities, Riverbend region

Community Liaison, Riverbend Regional Office. Declared record, 25 June 2026.

## Purpose of the visit

Community Liaison officers visited four remote communities in the north of the Riverbend region between 16 and 19 June 2026 to help flood-affected households apply for the Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant.

## What we found

We met 46 households. Most had lost furniture, bedding and white goods, and nine homes had serious structural damage.

Remote households often lack utility bills or leases in their own name, because power is prepaid through a community meter and housing is allocated informally through the community.

Several householders told us they had called the grant hotline and been told they could not apply without a utility bill.

## Recommendation

We recommend that the department accept statutory declarations or letters from community organisations as proof of address for households in remote communities.

This would bring the hotline into line with the Regional Office's existing practice. Community organisations in all four communities told us they are willing to write letters for residents and to keep a register of the letters they issue.

## Follow-up

Officers will return to the communities in August to help households that have not yet applied. A copy of this report has been sent to the Customer Service branch.
