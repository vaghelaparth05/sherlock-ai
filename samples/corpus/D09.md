# Staff FAQ summary: Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant

## Overview

The Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant supports households affected by the February 2026 floods in the Riverbend region. The grant is administered by the Department of Regional Recovery, and applications can be made online or through the grant hotline.

## Who is eligible?

To be eligible, a household must have a combined annual income below $90,000.

The property must be the household's main residence and must be in the flood-affected area.

## How much do households receive?

Every eligible household receives $5,000.

The payment is made once the application has been approved.

## How long does assessment take?

Most applications are assessed within a few weeks, although this may take longer during busy periods.

## What should staff tell callers?

Staff should explain the eligibility rules, help callers start an application and refer complex questions to a team leader. Staff should be friendly and patient, as many callers have been through a difficult experience.

## Where can I learn more?

More information is available on the Communications team site and from the Grants Policy branch.
