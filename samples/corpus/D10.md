# Proof of address procedure

Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant. Customer Service branch, declared procedure, 10 April 2026.

## Purpose

This procedure tells hotline and counter staff what evidence an applicant must provide to show that they lived at the damaged property at the time of the February 2026 floods.

## Requirement

Every applicant must provide a current utility bill or a lease showing the damaged property's address as proof of address.

The document must be in the applicant's name and must be dated within the six months before the floods. Electricity, gas, water and landline bills are accepted. A lease must be signed by both parties.

## Checking the document

Staff should check that the name and address on the document match the application. Where the document is unclear or damaged, ask the applicant to upload a clearer copy. Do not accept handwritten notes, screenshots of bank transactions or documents in another person's name.

## Applications without proof of address

An application cannot proceed to assessment until proof of address has been provided. Staff should explain this to the applicant and record the outcome of the call in the case management system.
