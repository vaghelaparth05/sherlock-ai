# Eligibility quick reference card

Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant. Customer Service branch, updated 20 May 2026 for Guidelines version 2.

Keep this card beside your screen during calls. It summarises the rules hotline staff are asked about most often.

## Income

To be eligible, a household must have a combined annual income below $120,000.

## Who can apply

The grant is available to owner-occupiers only.

## Proof of address

Applicants must provide proof of address in the form of a utility bill or a lease.

## Tips for the call

Confirm the address of the damaged property early in the call, because it must be inside the declared disaster area. If the caller does not know their exact household income, tell them to apply anyway and let the assessment officer check it. Do not promise a particular grant amount, because the amount depends on the damage assessment.

## When to escalate

Transfer the call to a team leader if the caller wants to complain, asks for a decision to be reviewed or reports suspected fraud. Record every call in the case management system before you close it.
