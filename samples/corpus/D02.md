# Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant Guidelines

Version 2. Issued by the Grants Policy branch, Department of Regional Recovery, 15 May 2026. This version replaces version 1 of 2 March 2026.

## What has changed

Following the decision of the Grants Steering Committee on 10 May 2026, the household income threshold has been raised to reflect cost-of-living pressures in the region. All other rules are unchanged.

## Eligibility

To be eligible, a household must have a combined annual income below $120,000.

The grant is available to owner-occupiers only.

The damaged property must be the household's principal place of residence and must be located within the declared Riverbend disaster area.

## Grant amounts

The grant is paid in three tiers: $2,000 for loss of contents, $5,000 for minor structural damage and $10,000 for major structural damage.

The tier is set by an assessment officer after reviewing photographs, receipts and, where needed, an inspection of the property.

## Applications

Applications close on 31 October 2026.

Applicants must provide proof of address in the form of a utility bill or a lease.

No fee is charged to apply for the grant or to have an application assessed.

Approved grants are paid by electronic funds transfer into the applicant's own bank account.

## Review

Applicants who disagree with a decision may request an internal review within 28 days. These guidelines will be reviewed in May 2027, or earlier if the program changes.
