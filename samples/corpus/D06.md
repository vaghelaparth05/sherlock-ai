# Media release: Flood recovery grants open for Riverbend households

Issued by the Minister's office, 1 March 2026.

The Minister for Regional Recovery today announced a new grant to help Riverbend households get back on their feet after the February floods.

"Families across Riverbend have lost homes, furniture and livelihoods. This grant is about getting them practical help, quickly," the Minister said.

Eligible households will receive $2,000 to replace lost contents, $5,000 where the home has minor structural damage, or $10,000 where the damage is major.

The Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant will be administered by the Department of Regional Recovery. Applications can be made online or by calling the grant hotline.

Households have until 31 October 2026 to lodge an application.

The Minister thanked emergency services, volunteers and local councils for their work during and after the floods, and encouraged anyone affected to check whether they are eligible.

The grant forms part of the broader Riverbend recovery package, which also includes support for small businesses, primary producers and local infrastructure. Further announcements about the package will be made in the coming weeks.

Media enquiries should be directed to the media unit in the Minister's office.
