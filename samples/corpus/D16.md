# Executive board briefing: Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant

Prepared for the Executive Board meeting of 5 June 2026.

## Purpose

To update the Board on the progress of the Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant and to seek its agreement to the planned introduction of an AI assistant for call-centre staff.

## Program status

Since the program opened in March, the department has received 8,400 applications and approved 5,100 grants. The average time from application to payment is 24 days.

Under the current guidelines, households earning less than $120,000 a year are eligible for the grant.

The closing date for applications remains 31 October 2026, and no extension is proposed at this stage.

## AI assistant for the hotline

The department plans to give its 400 call-centre staff an AI assistant from 1 July 2026 to help answer questions about the grant. The Chief Information Officer has asked the information governance team to confirm which sources the assistant should be allowed to read before it goes live.

## Risks

Demand on the hotline is expected to peak in October as the closing date approaches. Scam activity targeting applicants has also increased, and Internal Audit is reviewing payment controls.

## Recommendation

That the Board note the program status and agree to the AI assistant going live on 1 July 2026, subject to the information governance review.
