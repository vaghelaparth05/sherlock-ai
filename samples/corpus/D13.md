From: Regional Operations, Riverbend Regional Office

To: Customer Service branch, Head Office

Date: 22 June 2026

Subject: Remote applicants being turned away by the hotline

Hi team,

I want to raise a pattern we have seen over the past three weeks. Several households from remote communities have come to the Riverbend Regional Office after calling the grant hotline. Each of them said they were told they could not apply because they did not have a utility bill or lease in their own name.

In at least eleven cases this month, hotline staff turned away remote applicants who had no utility bill.

Some of these households lost everything in the floods and are now living with relatives. Several had driven more than two hours to reach our office.

Our declared remote community applications procedure already covers these households, but it sits in the regional records system, which hotline staff cannot see. The hotline scripts and the quick reference card do not mention it.

Could we meet this week to work out how to get the procedure in front of hotline staff before the department's AI assistant goes live on 1 July? We are happy to brief team leaders by video, and we can share the case details if that would help.

Thanks,

Regional Operations, Riverbend Regional Office
