# Staff newsletter: June 2026

Corporate Services branch, Department of Regional Recovery.

## From the Corporate Services desk

Welcome to the June edition of the staff newsletter. It has been a huge few months for everyone at the department, and we want to say thank you for the long hours many of you have put in since the floods.

## Flood recovery grant program

The Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant program launched in March and has now paid more than 5,000 grants to local households. Teams across Head Office and the Riverbend Regional Office have worked together to make it happen.

Applications for the grant close on 31 October 2026, so expect the hotline to stay busy through spring.

## New faces

We welcome twelve new hotline officers who joined the Customer Service branch this month, and two new graduates in Finance.

## Building news

The third-floor kitchen will be closed for renovations from 22 to 26 June. Please use the kitchens on the second and fourth floors.

## Wellbeing

The Employee Assistance Program is free and confidential for all staff and their families. Flu vaccinations are also available in the health room on Thursdays this month.

## Social club

The end-of-financial-year trivia night is on Friday 26 June. All welcome.
