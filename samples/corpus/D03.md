# Call centre script: Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant

Prepared by the Customer Service branch for grant hotline staff, March 2026.

## Opening

Thank the caller for contacting the Department of Regional Recovery and confirm they are calling about the Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant. Ask for the caller's name, a contact number and the address of the damaged property.

## Checking eligibility

Read the eligibility rule to the caller as written.

To be eligible, a household must have a combined annual income below $90,000.

If the caller is unsure of their household income, explain that the assessment officer will check it and that they should still apply.

## Deadline

Applications close on 31 October 2026.

Encourage callers to apply early, because assessment can take up to six weeks during busy periods.

## Ending the call

Offer to send the application link by text message or email. Remind the caller to keep receipts and photographs of the damage, as these help the assessment officer. If the caller is distressed, offer the number for the recovery support line.

## Escalation

Transfer the call to a team leader if the caller wishes to make a complaint or asks about a decision that has already been made. Record every call in the case management system before closing it.
