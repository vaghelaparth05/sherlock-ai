# Internal audit memo: Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant controls

From Internal Audit to the Audit Committee, 10 June 2026.

## Purpose

This memo reports the results of Internal Audit's review of payment controls for the Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant, covering payments made between 1 April and 31 May 2026.

## Findings

We tested a sample of 120 approved applications against the grant guidelines and the Finance payments procedure.

The department does not charge applicants any fee to apply, to be assessed or to be paid.

All sampled payments were made by electronic funds transfer to an account in the applicant's own name, and no payment was made to a third party.

Each sampled payment matched an approved tier, being $2,000 where only contents were lost, $5,000 for minor structural repairs or $10,000 for major structural repairs.

One minor exception was identified. Two payments were released before the bank account name had been checked. Both accounts were later confirmed as belonging to the applicants.

## Risks

We note an increase in scam messages targeting flood-affected households, including messages that ask for upfront payments. Staff should continue to tell callers that the department never asks applicants to pay money or to send funds to any account.

## Recommendations

Finance should add a system check that blocks payment until the account name has been confirmed. Customer Service should brief hotline staff on how to recognise and report scam messages, and should record any reports in the case management system.

Management has accepted both recommendations, with completion due by 31 July 2026.
