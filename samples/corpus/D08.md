# Payments procedure: Riverbend Flood Recovery Grant

Finance branch, Department of Regional Recovery. Declared procedure, 18 May 2026.

## Scope

This procedure covers how approved Riverbend Flood Recovery Grants are paid. It applies to Finance officers and to assessment officers who approve payments.

## Payment amounts

The approved amount is one of the three grant tiers: contents ($2,000), minor structural ($5,000) or major structural ($10,000).

Finance officers must not pay any other amount. If an assessment records an amount that does not match a tier, return it to the assessment officer for correction.

## Fees

Applicants are never charged a fee at any stage of the application, assessment or payment process.

## How grants are paid

Grants are paid only by electronic funds transfer into a bank account held in the applicant's own name.

Finance officers must confirm that the account name matches the applicant before releasing a payment. Payments to third parties, agents or accounts in another person's name are not permitted.

The department will never ask an applicant to transfer money to any account, and staff must treat any such request as suspected fraud.

## Records

Every payment is recorded in the financial management system with a reference to the approved assessment. Payment records are kept in the department's records system in line with the relevant disposal authority.

## Reporting suspected fraud

Report suspected fraud to Internal Audit on the same day it is identified, and do not release any further payment on the affected application until Internal Audit has responded.
